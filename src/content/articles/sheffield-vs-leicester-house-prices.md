# Sheffield vs Leicester House Prices: 2000–2025 Comparison

Compare Sheffield and Leicester average sold prices from 2000 to 2025 using HM Land Registry Price Paid data. This analysis uses annual district-level transaction counts and arithmetic mean prices from the supplied filtered Land Registry export.

## Key findings

In **Sheffield**, the mean recorded sale price was **£66,479 in 2000** and **£262,978 in 2025**. That is a **295.6%** change across the period. The 2025 annual mean was **+1.9%** relative to 2024. There were **6,536** qualifying transactions in the 2025 extract.

In **Leicester**, the mean recorded sale price was **£57,715 in 2000** and **£255,869 in 2025**. That is a **343.3%** change across the period. The 2025 annual mean was **+0.6%** relative to 2024. There were **2,360** qualifying transactions in the 2025 extract.

In 2025, **Sheffield** averaged **£262,978** and **Leicester** averaged **£255,869**. The difference (Sheffield minus Leicester) was **£7,109**, with **Sheffield** higher.

## Methodology and limitations

- Source: user-supplied annual export derived from HM Land Registry Price Paid transactions.
- Geographic level: Land Registry district names as recorded in the export, not local authority boundary-normalised comparisons.
- Years: calendar years 2000–2025 inclusive.
- Filters applied when producing the supplied extract: `PPDCategoryType = A`, `PropertyType IN (D, S, T, F)`, with mean sale price calculated across included transactions.
- Average prices are arithmetic means, not medians or repeat-sales indices; no inflation adjustment.
- 2025 data may be affected by registration lag or later revisions.
- This is descriptive historical analysis, not an investment forecast or valuation.
