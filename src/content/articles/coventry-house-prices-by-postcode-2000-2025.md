## Highest prices are not necessarily the highest growth

CV7's higher overall average sold price reflects the prices of the properties transacted there; it does not automatically imply stronger historical growth. Likewise, a lower starting average can produce a larger percentage increase without delivering better rental yields or total returns. Neither ranking accounts for purchase costs, mortgage interest, rental income, maintenance, tax, inflation or selling costs.

## What should property investors take from this?

For anyone researching Coventry property investment, postcode-level growth is a starting point rather than a buying decision. Compare similar property types, investigate transaction volumes and local streets, and separately assess rents and ongoing costs. A citywide or postcode-wide average can conceal substantial differences between individual neighbourhoods.

## Data source and methodology

- **Source:** HM Land Registry Price Paid Data, aggregated from the site's SQL reporting dataset.
- **Geography:** postcode districts CV1, CV2, CV3, CV4, CV5, CV6 and CV7. **These postcode districts do not exactly match Coventry City Council's boundary**; notably CV7 includes locations outside the city.
- **Period:** calendar years **2000 and 2025** for endpoint comparisons, with annual trend data from 2000 through 2025. The available export also contains partial 2026 observations, which are excluded.
- **All-property averages:** calculated as total sale value divided by the number of transactions across detached, semi-detached, terraced and flat/maisonette records, using transaction counts and category means from the export.
- **Terraced medians:** annual median sold prices for property type T, as supplied by the SQL export. The percentage change is `(2025 median / 2000 median âˆ’ 1) Ã— 100`.
- **Limitations:** no adjustment for inflation, transaction composition, property quality or repeat sales; these are **nominal** figures. The export begins in 2000, so it does not support a 1995â€“2025 claim. Results depend on the underlying reporting view's filtering and coverage.
