# Oxford vs Coventry House Prices: 2000–2025 Comparison

Compare Oxford and Coventry average sold prices from 2000 to 2025 using HM Land Registry Price Paid data. This analysis uses annual district-level transaction counts and arithmetic mean prices from the supplied filtered Land Registry export.

## Key findings

In **Oxford**, the mean recorded sale price was **£167,985 in 2000** and **£585,850 in 2025**. That is a **248.8%** change across the period. The 2025 annual mean was **-2.4%** relative to 2024. There were **1,414** qualifying transactions in the 2025 extract.

In **Coventry**, the mean recorded sale price was **£65,108 in 2000** and **£254,514 in 2025**. That is a **290.9%** change across the period. The 2025 annual mean was **+4.7%** relative to 2024. There were **3,739** qualifying transactions in the 2025 extract.

In 2025, **Oxford** averaged **£585,850** and **Coventry** averaged **£254,514**. The difference (Oxford minus Coventry) was **£331,336**, with **Oxford** higher.

## Methodology and limitations

- Source: user-supplied annual export derived from HM Land Registry Price Paid transactions.
- Geographic level: Land Registry district names as recorded in the export, not local authority boundary-normalised comparisons.
- Years: calendar years 2000–2025 inclusive.
- Filters applied when producing the supplied extract: `PPDCategoryType = A`, `PropertyType IN (D, S, T, F)`, with mean sale price calculated across included transactions.
- Average prices are arithmetic means, not medians or repeat-sales indices; no inflation adjustment.
- 2025 data may be affected by registration lag or later revisions.
- This is descriptive historical analysis, not an investment forecast or valuation.
